## Cinematic hero loop

`a-little-mystery.mp4` and `a-little-mystery-poster.webp` are public, non-sensitive generated marketing assets created on 20 October 2026 through the Higgsfield connector using Wan 2.6. They are abstract brand artwork, not user captures or evidence of app privacy. The generation requested one ten-second, 16:9, 1080p clip and was quoted at 20 credits with that submission capped at 20. The earlier Seedance 2.5 attempt was rejected for its Plus-plan requirement and returned no job.

The source was generated at 1920×1080. Website delivery uses a silent H.264 1600×900 MP4 with fast-start metadata. Unwanted generated lettering was cropped out; the art edge is feathered into a dark canvas. The first five seconds run forward and then backward to form a ten-second loop. The poster comes from the final delivered video. The original provider download remains in ignored local cache rather than source control.
